The NLP Data Pipeline — From Text to Word Vectors

Model: NLP Preprocessing & EmbeddingFlow: raw text → 300-dim vectors
The Limits of One-Hot Encoding

Every word adds its own huge dimension (tens of thousands), and an almost-all-zero sparse matrix explodes. It also cannot measure at all how similar '얼음' (ice) and '아이스' (iced) are.

The Word2Vec Breakthrough

It compresses words into fixed low-dimensional dense vectors (300 dims) and learns to pull words that appear in similar contexts close together in vector space.

Preserve meaning with 300-dim dense Skip-gram vectors

One-hot encoding: with 10,000 words it builds 10,000-dim vectors like [0, 0, 1, ..., 0], so memory explodes. Word2Vec expresses meaning relations with 300 continuous real values.

Why Do We Need Word Vectors?

  1. Beating the curse of dimensionality: even as vocabulary grows, the vector dimension stays fixed (e.g. 300), maximizing the compute efficiency of machine learning models.
  2. Similarity becomes computable: one-hot vectors always multiply to 0 (orthogonal), so word similarity is unknowable; word vectors use cosine similarity to show that '작대기' (slang for syringe) and '주사기' (syringe) share a context.

Paper Takeaway: Why Keyword Filtering Fails

Normal posts also say "더운 여름 아이스 아메리카노!" ("iced americano on a hot summer day!"), so banning the word '아이스' (iced) alone blocks whole normal conversations — a false positive. Criminals exploit exactly this: they hide slang inside contexts identical to ordinary commerce.

Dissecting the Disguise — The Secrets of XAI and LIME

Model: LIME & SHAPField: investigation evidence and interpretation

💡 What Is LIME? — The Doctor's Flu Diagnosis Analogy (Paper Figure 3)

When a complex AI (black box) rules that a post is a "drug deal", this technique finds out why by deleting or swapping one word at a time and testing how the result changes.

[The doctor's flu diagnosis]
• Sneezing present (+contribution)
• Headache present (+contribution)
• No fatigue (no contribution)
→ "I diagnosed flu because of the headache and sneezing."
[The AI's drug-post ruling]
• "당일가능" (same-day OK) present (+contribution)
• "에이드/20만원" ("aid" / 200,000 won) present (+contribution)
• "베스트셀러" (best-seller) absent (=0) (+contribution)
→ "Flagged due to disguise words and the absence of normal commerce words."
The Criminal's Guess

"Mix in normal commerce words like '특가세일' (special sale), '당일가능' and '에이드', and the AI will mistake it for an ordinary shop post, right?"

XAI's Catch

LIME and SHAP compute that the very absence (=0) of must-have normal-commerce words (set sales, best-seller) is powerful evidence of crime.

Interactive XAI Feature-Contribution Bars

Red bars are features contributing positively (+) to the drug-deal ruling. The combination — and absence — of normal words is the key to detection.
In the field: when an investigator requests a search warrant, they can submit explainability as evidence — "the abnormal combined weight of '에이드' and '20만원' in this post acted at +0.28" — instead of "the AI just said it is drugs."
